About this policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to AustroPlus VPN (Android package: xyz.tekidoer.austroplus.vpn), provided by the independent developer operating under the Tekidoer name (“we”, “us” or “our”). It describes information that may be processed by the app, its integrated software development kits (SDKs), and the VPN infrastructure you choose to connect to.

We aim to collect and use only information needed to provide, secure, maintain and improve the app, deliver advertising where enabled, and provide support. The exact information processed can depend on the app version, enabled features, your consent choices, Android settings and the selected VPN server.

Important: A VPN does not make you completely anonymous. Your internet traffic is routed through the VPN server you select. The server operator and the websites or services you visit may process information as part of providing their services.

This policy should be read together with the privacy information and consent choices shown inside the app and the privacy policies of the third-party providers described below.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on how you use the app and which services are enabled, the following categories may be processed:

  • App and device information: app version, Android version, device model, language, device or installation identifiers, and technical information needed for compatibility, security and service delivery.
  • Network information: IP address, network type, connection status, selected server, connection timestamps, protocol and technical connection diagnostics. Some of this information is visible to the VPN server or network providers as part of routing a connection.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), ad interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, diagnostics and app interaction information processed by Google Mobile Ads or its partners when ads are enabled.
  • Push notification information: push token or subscription identifier, app/device information, notification delivery status and notification interaction events if OneSignal or Firebase Cloud Messaging features are enabled.
  • Support information: information you choose to send us, such as your email address, message, screenshots or diagnostic details.
  • Configuration information: settings, server preferences and connection profiles you enter or import. Avoid including passwords, private keys or other secrets in support messages.

We do not intend to collect the content of your communications or browsing activity for advertising. However, the VPN infrastructure necessarily routes network traffic, and the server or hosting operator may process connection metadata or traffic information according to its own configuration and policies.

3. VPN connections and network traffic

When you activate the VPN, Android's VPN service routes the traffic covered by your selected configuration through the VPN tunnel and the selected remote server. The server must process network packets and connection information to deliver the service.

  • The VPN server may receive your public IP address, connection time, protocol and technical session information.
  • The destination websites and online services you access may receive the VPN server's IP address and may independently collect information about your visit.
  • DNS requests may be handled by the DNS resolver configured by the app, server or connection profile.
  • Custom server addresses, payloads, SNI, proxy settings, credentials or configuration files that you enter are used to establish the connection you request.

We do not make a blanket promise that no connection logs are ever created. Logging and retention can depend on the selected server, hosting provider, protocol and operational configuration. Please review any separate terms or privacy information provided by the VPN server operator where applicable.

Disconnecting the VPN stops the app's VPN tunnel, but does not delete records that may already have been created by a server, network provider, advertising provider or notification provider.

4. Advertising and consent (Google AdMob / UMP)

Where advertising is enabled, the app may use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, rewarded or app-open advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as IP address, advertising or app identifiers, app interactions, ad views/clicks, diagnostic information and approximate location inferred from IP address for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and service operation.

We may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent or privacy-choice messages where required. Your available choices depend on your region, applicable law and Google's consent tools. You can revisit privacy choices through any privacy options entry point made available in the app, and manage advertising ID settings through Android settings where supported.

Advertising providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob privacy and data disclosure.

5. Firebase, app services and push notifications

Depending on the build and features enabled, the app may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications and Firebase App Check to help protect app-related backend resources. FCM and its supporting Firebase components may process app version, installation identifiers, device/app technical information, push tokens and delivery-related data. App Check may process app/device integrity signals or attestation information to help verify that requests come from an authentic app environment.

OneSignal may also be integrated in builds where push notifications are enabled. In that case, OneSignal may process a push subscription identifier, device and app information, notification delivery data and interaction events to provide notification services.

These services are not necessarily enabled in every version or build. The precise information processed depends on SDK versions, configuration and features actually used. See Firebase privacy information, Firebase data disclosure and OneSignal Privacy Policy.

6. How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared or processed in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Google (AdMob, UMP and Firebase), OneSignal, VPN server/hosting operators, and infrastructure providers where required to deliver app functions.
  • Security and legal requirements: where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, enforce applicable terms, comply with a lawful request or meet legal obligations.
  • At your direction: when you choose to send information to a third party, connect to a server, or use an external service.

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Some providers or VPN servers may operate in countries outside your own, where data protection laws may differ.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including app operation, security, support, legal compliance and dispute resolution. Retention periods vary by information type and service provider. We do not state a fixed retention period for VPN server logs because this may depend on the independently operated server and its configuration.

App settings and locally stored configuration can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup settings. This does not erase information already held by third-party providers or VPN server operators.

To ask about information associated with a support request or to request deletion of information we control, email tekidoervpndev@gmail.com and describe the app and information concerned. We may need to verify your request. We will handle valid requests subject to applicable law and information we are legally required or permitted to retain.

If a version of the app offers account creation, account deletion must also be available through the applicable in-app or web process. At present, this policy does not claim that an account system exists in every version.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the app's operation. These may include encrypted network transport where supported by the relevant service, access restrictions and secure configuration practices. No internet transmission, device, VPN tunnel or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure and protecting any VPN credentials, private keys, subscription links and custom configuration details you use. Do not share these secrets with people you do not trust.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at tekidoervpndev@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps where required by law. App availability and advertising settings must also comply with the age and audience declarations made in Google Play Console.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. South African users may have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA); other local laws may provide additional rights.

  • Use the consent and privacy controls presented in the app where available.
  • Manage your Android advertising ID and notification permissions in device settings.
  • Disconnect from the VPN whenever you choose.
  • Contact us by email to submit a privacy request or ask a question.

We may be unable to fulfil a request concerning data controlled independently by Google, OneSignal, a VPN server operator or another third party. In those cases, you may need to contact that provider directly.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the app, its SDKs, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.
